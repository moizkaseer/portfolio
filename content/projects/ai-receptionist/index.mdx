## The problem

Small clinics in North Macedonia answer their phone and WhatsApp by hand, in whichever of four languages a caller happens to use, and a missed call during a treatment is a missed booking. I wanted a receptionist a dental clinic could trust with its FAQs and its calendar without it ever inventing a price or a policy, in Macedonian, Albanian, Serbian or English.

## Decisions & tradeoffs

- **The model decides, code validates.** The LLM never writes to the database; a tool call is checked (dates in the future, inside opening hours, no double booking, phone format) before anything executes, and only the validated code path touches Postgres.
- **Grounded-only FAQ answers.** The agent answers from the clinic's own knowledge base, injected into the prompt. A missing topic becomes a message for the owner, not a guess.
- **Disclosure at the start, in the caller's language.** Every conversation opens by stating it's an AI assistant and that the conversation may be recorded — for EU AI Act transparency and GDPR's recording notice. The production system prompt has that line written out in all four languages.
- **Handoff triggers are code rules, not the model's self-confidence.** A medical emergency, an angry caller, two failed tool validations in a row, or a knowledge-base miss asked twice all go straight to a human handoff, and the code-level check runs before the model is even asked.
- **Double booking is blocked by the database, not only the prompt.** `bookings` has a Postgres exclusion constraint on business + staff + time range, so an overlapping confirmed booking for the same staff cannot be inserted, independent of whatever the model decides to do.
- **Multi-tenant from day one.** Every table carries a business ID, so a second clinic is a new row, not a new deployment.
- **Replayable runs.** Every turn stores the prompt version, model, knowledge-base hash and tool I/O, so a conversation can be replayed later and diffed against a new prompt or model.

## What's hard / risks

- WhatsApp Business Cloud API verification with Meta can take days, and replies outside the 24-hour window need pre-approved templates — this needs to start early, before Phase 2 is otherwise ready.
- Local Twilio numbers may not be available in North Macedonia; the likely workaround is a foreign number with call forwarding from the clinic's own line.
- Macedonian and Albanian speech recognition quality is unmeasured — Phase 3 (voice) will measure it, not assume it.
- Keyword-based emergency detection is a safety net, not a guarantee; the keyword list and the clinic's emergency number still need a clinic-side review.

## What's next

- **Phase 2 — WhatsApp text version.** The webhook, the conversation loop, the six agent tools, argument validation, logging.
- **Phase 3 — Voice.** Twilio for calls, ElevenLabs for speech-to-text and text-to-speech.
- **Phase 4 — Admin UI.** A Next.js + TypeScript app for editing the knowledge base, managing bookings and reading transcripts.
- **Phase 5 — Evaluation.** 20 scripted conversations across the four languages, scored by an eval script.
