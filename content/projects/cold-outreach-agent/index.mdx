## The problem

Sales reps know a researched email beats a template, but researching by hand is slow, so most teams send templates. Asking a model to "personalise it" is fast, but it invents facts. One made-up funding round in a cold email costs the sender their credibility. I wanted the speed of a model with the honesty of a person who actually read the page.

## Decisions & tradeoffs

- **Code makes the final call.** The pipeline is a plain Python state machine. A fact is kept only if its URL was fetched in this run and its quote appears in the page text. The verifier maps every claim in the email to a fact, and code decides pass or fail.
- **One autonomous agent, with no dangerous tools.** Only the research step loops on its own. It has three tools, and none of them can send, write or delete. Fetched pages are wrapped as untrusted data.
- **A hand-written agent loop on the Anthropic SDK**, with no framework, so cost, retries and turn limits stay visible.
- **Cheap checks first.** A poor-fit lead is rejected for about $0.0013, before any research is paid for.
- **A person approves every email.** An opt-out in a reply is caught by plain code, even if the model is down. An email is claimed in one conditional `UPDATE` before sending, so it goes out at most once.

## What broke

- **240 tests passed, and the API could not start.** The tests injected their own dependencies and never ran real startup. Now a smoke test boots the real server, worker, Postgres and Redis.
- **The LLM judge said 92% faithful; reading the facts myself said about 75%.** Changing only the judge moved the same 24 facts from 0.92 to 0.67. I rebuilt it so the model names a quote for each detail, and code derives the verdict.
- **A rate limit let 5 of 6 requests through.** "Check, then create" is two steps. A Postgres advisory lock makes it exactly one of six.

## What's next

One real email through the provider, measuring the reply classifier, a review screen, and a fit signal such as "do they already use a competitor?". The sample is still small. How real sales people rate the emails, and reply rates, are not measured yet.
